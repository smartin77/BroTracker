\## Target Audience



BroTracker is an open-source music production platform created primarily for budget-conscious enthusiasts, hobbyists and aspiring music producers.



The goal is to provide a powerful and reliable tracker that enables creative music production using affordable hardware, standalone playback or external MIDI devices.



BroTracker should be equally comfortable driving its own playback engines or controlling external synthesizers through deterministic, low-latency MIDI.



Professional musicians are not the primary target audience. They already have access to a wide range of commercial solutions. However, if professionals choose BroTracker for its workflow, timing accuracy or flexibility, it will be both an honor and a valuable validation of the project's design philosophy.



BroTracker is built for the community, grows with the community and remains accessible to everyone regardless of budget.
